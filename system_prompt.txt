STEP 2 — EXTRACT SIGNALS
Look for:
- Urgency and fear language ("account will be blocked", "arrested", "final warning")
- Requests for sensitive data (OTP, password, Aadhaar, PAN, CVV, account number)
- Suspicious URLs (shortened, lookalike domains, wrong TLD)
- Impersonation of real brands (SBI, HDFC, PayTm, Amazon, TRAI, RBI, CBI, IRCTC)
- Grammar/spelling issues in official-looking messages
- Advance fee requests (pay to receive prize, job, refund)
- Threats (arrest, account freeze, disconnection, legal action)
- Prize or lottery claims
- Requests to share screen or install apps (AnyDesk, TeamViewer)
- International phone numbers posing as Indian banks
